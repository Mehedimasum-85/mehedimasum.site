MY STORY

My Creative Journey

I started working on graphic design projects in 2022 and built my early experience as a freelancer, taking on design projects through online creative platforms.

In December 2023 I joined LogicalTBX as a Graphic Designer, working on social media creatives and print materials. In June 2024 I moved to Softvence, where I grew into a Team Lead role in January 2025. There I worked across branding, marketing creatives, campaign visuals and portfolio presentations, while coordinating the creative team.

Most recently I worked as an Executive - Graphics Designer in the Creative Services Department at Adency Limited, focused on marketing creatives, campaign visuals and print designs.
